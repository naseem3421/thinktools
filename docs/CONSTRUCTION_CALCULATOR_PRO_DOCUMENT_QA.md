## 3. Remediation Details for Incomplete Resources

### 3.1 Bonus 1: Contractor Bill Check
- **Identified Issue:** Header displayed `"DRAFT. Check every point against local practice and your contract before sale."`
- **Correction Made:**
  - Subtitle updated to: `"Administrative Verification Protocol • Companion to Tab 25 (Bill Check)"`
  - Re-structured into four actionable sections: *What It Solves*, *How To Use It Step-by-Step*, *Common Technical Causes of Variance*, and *Recommended Verification Inquiries*.
  - Added explicit technical boundary: *"This checklist verifies physical material quantities. Unit rates and labour charges should be audited independently using Tab 20 (Rates) and prevailing market benchmarks."*
  - Recompiled as single-page A4 document with high-clarity typography.

### 3.2 Bonus 2: Payment Schedule Guide
- **Identified Issue:** Header displayed `"DRAFT. This is general guidance, not legal advice. Check the terms with a lawyer before you use them."`
- **Correction Made:**
  - Subtitle updated to: `"Milestone Cash Flow Framework • Administrative Planning Reference (Not Legal Advice)"`
  - Articulated core milestone gates (Substructure / Plinth, RCC Slabs, Masonry & Plaster, Joinery & Finishes, Final Handover).
  - Defined explicit retention threshold (5%–10% withheld until defect liability walkthrough).
  - Clarified legal nature: standard administrative framework requiring customization to local contract terms.

### 3.3 Bonus 3: Change Order Control
- **Identified Issue:** Header displayed `"DRAFT. Check the wording with a lawyer before you use it in a contract."`
- **Correction Made:**
  - Subtitle updated to: `"Site Variation Management Procedure • Standard Administrative Form (Not Legal Advice)"`
  - Established the Golden Site Rule: *"No additional or modified work shall commence on site until the variation is documented in writing, priced with applicable rates, and signed by both owner and contractor."*
  - Formalized standard field items: Change Order ID, Technical Reason, Detailed Scope, Net Rupee Impact, Programme Impact (+/- days), Dual Signatures.
  - Linked directly to Tab 28 (`Payments`) change order register.

### 3.4 Combined Master Guide (`Construction_Calculator_Pro_Guide_COMBINED.pdf`)
